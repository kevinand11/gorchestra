# Delivery and Slice dependency boundaries

Gorchestra supports `depends-on` Links at two execution-relevant levels with different scopes. Delivery-to-Delivery dependencies are hard scheduling gates: if Delivery B depends on Delivery A, no Slice in Delivery B may begin until Delivery A is Shipped or Abandoned. Abandoned Deliveries satisfy dependency calculation so obsolete or no-longer-pursued prerequisite work does not permanently block dependent Deliveries; abandoning a queued Delivery must also close or abandon its active external work where possible. Delivery dependency Links must be acyclic. Slice-to-Slice dependencies are limited to Slices within the same Delivery, order that Delivery's internal execution, and must be acyclic within that Delivery. Cross-Delivery ordering belongs at the Delivery level rather than through cross-Delivery Slice Links, keeping execution scheduling understandable at both the Delivery and Slice levels while avoiding cross-Delivery Slice dependency complexity.
