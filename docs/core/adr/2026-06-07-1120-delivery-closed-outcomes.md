# Delivery closed outcomes

A Delivery has at most one closed outcome, represented by a ship-delivery or abandon-delivery Action. Shipped and Abandoned are both closed, dependency-satisfying outcomes, and core rejects attempts to record a second closed Action for the same Delivery. queueDelivery, shipDelivery, and abandonDelivery are non-idempotent: once the corresponding Action exists, repeating the command fails rather than creating another Action or returning the previous one. A Delivery may Ship only after all of its Slices are complete and its Delivery Artifact is integrated into the target. For Source Control Projects, integration may be observed through a merged Delivery Review Surface or by observing that the Target Branch already contains the Delivery Branch before creating a new Delivery Review Surface. V1 does not run post-merge Ship validation; post-merge validation is a future feature candidate. A Delivery may be Abandoned before all Slices are complete, but core records the abandon-delivery Action only after required Project Source Type-specific cleanup is attempted or recorded in that Action. This prevents races or retries from making a Delivery appear both successfully integrated and intentionally no longer pursued, prevents partially completed accepted work from being shipped, and keeps abandonment tied to external cleanup responsibilities while preserving Actions as the Delivery state-transition timeline.
