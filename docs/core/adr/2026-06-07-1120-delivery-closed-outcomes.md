# Delivery closed outcomes

A Delivery has at most one closed outcome. Shipped and Abandoned are both closed, dependency-satisfying outcomes, and core rejects attempts to record a second closed outcome for the same Delivery. A Delivery may Ship only after all of its Slices are complete and its Delivery Artifact is integrated into the target. For Source Control Projects, integration may be observed through a merged Delivery Review Surface or by observing that the Target Branch already contains the Delivery Branch before creating a new Delivery Review Surface. V1 does not run post-merge Ship validation; post-merge validation is a future feature candidate. A Delivery may be Abandoned before all Slices are complete, but core records Abandoned only after required Project Source Type-specific cleanup is attempted or recorded. This prevents races or retries from making a Delivery appear both successfully integrated and intentionally no longer pursued, prevents partially completed accepted work from being shipped, and keeps abandonment tied to external cleanup responsibilities.
