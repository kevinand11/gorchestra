# Delivery closed outcomes

A Delivery has at most one closed outcome, represented by its `closed` lifecycle field. Shipped and Abandoned are both closed, dependency-satisfying outcomes, and core rejects attempts to replace or record a second closed outcome for the same Delivery. queueDelivery, shipDelivery, and abandonDelivery are non-idempotent: once the corresponding lifecycle field exists, repeating the command fails rather than returning the previous Delivery. A Delivery may Ship only after all of its Slices are complete and its Delivery Artifact is integrated into the target. For Source Control Projects, integration may be observed through a merged Delivery Review Surface or by observing that the Target Branch already contains the Delivery Branch before creating a new Review Surface. V1 does not run post-merge Ship validation; post-merge validation is a future feature candidate. A Delivery may be Abandoned before all Slices are complete, but core records the abandoned closed outcome only after required Project Source Type-specific cleanup is attempted or encoded in the closed lifecycle record. This prevents races or retries from making a Delivery appear both successfully integrated and intentionally no longer pursued, prevents partially completed accepted work from being shipped, and keeps lifecycle gates readable from the Delivery record while Actions remain execution facts rather than lifecycle authority.
